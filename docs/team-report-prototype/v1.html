How it would work

  • Team report (new button next to Yearly): the left list has Team at the top, then each person grouped by their role on the Team page.
  • Each person's report is only about their role. Content writer → content written, approved, sent back, edits. Graphic designer → designs delivered, on time, revisions, creatives uploaded. Video editor → videos delivered (reels, YouTube), on time, revisions, turnaround. Nothing from other roles.
  • Team puts everyone side by side, then one section per role — so all designers' work is in one place, all editors' in another.
  • Pick a month at the top (like the Monthly report). Each month is saved at month end, so old months open instantly; a year view adds the months up.
  • Every table has Copy table, and each report has Add a section for notes.
  • Who sees what: admins see everyone; each person sees only their own report (switch on Team & access).
  • Numbers come from what people already do in the dashboard (status changes, uploads, dates) — nothing new to fill in.
