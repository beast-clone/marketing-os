How it would work

  1. Pick a month from the box, or step with the arrows. The link remembers the month, so you can send someone “October”.
  2. This month is live, from the 1st to today.
  3. On the last day of every month at 11:55 PM the dashboard saves the whole report on its own, so every month is kept in full even if nobody opens it.
  4. Past months show that month-end copy. Followers and subscribers can only be counted on the day, so they come from the copy. Each number shows where it came from.
  5. March–May come from the Notion report we imported. June–August were never saved, so only leads exist for them.
  6. Each month keeps its own Achievements and Future Prospects notes.
